A FEW LITTLE DETAILS

Before your
moment.

Can I combine different treatments?+

Of course. Add your favourites from different categories to your visit. The menu totals their example prices and durations, then shows times that fit the full appointment.

What if I’m new to lashes or brows?+

Your studio will advise whether a patch test or consultation is needed, and how far in advance to arrange it. A demo booking does not replace that check.

How do aesthetics appointments work?+

Start with a consultation to discuss your goals, suitability and available options, including dermal fillers where appropriate. Any treatment is a separate decision following an individual assessment.

Will this book a real appointment?+

This is an interactive sample studio. You can explore treatments, choose a day and save a demo visit locally. Live availability, payments, confirmations and cancellation policies will be supplied when your studio’s booking system is connected.
