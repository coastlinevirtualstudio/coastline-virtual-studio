Privacy Notice

Last updated: 19 August 2026

Coastline Virtual Studio (“we”, “us”) is run by Tayla Palmer and is based in Selsey, West Sussex. This notice explains how we collect, use and look after your personal information.

Email: sales@coastlinevirtualstudio.co.uk

Website: www.coastlinevirtualstudio.co.uk

What information we collect

We may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Business name and details of your enquiry
  • Messages you send via our website, email, Facebook or Instagram
  • Information from Facebook lead forms or adverts, if you submit them
  • Basic website usage information (for example pages visited), if cookies or analytics are used

We only collect what we need to respond to you and provide our services.

How we collect it

  • Our website contact form
  • Email, phone or social media messages
  • Facebook or Instagram enquiries and lead forms
  • Google Business Profile messages
  • When you become a client and we work together

Why we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to enquiries
  • Provide website design, virtual assistance, admin and marketing services
  • Send quotes, invoices and project updates
  • Improve our website and services
  • Meet legal and accounting requirements

Lawful bases (UK GDPR)

  • Contract — to provide services you have asked for
  • Legitimate interests — to respond to enquiries and run the business
  • Consent — where you have asked us to contact you, or for optional marketing
  • Legal obligation — for invoices, tax and records we must keep

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We may share it only with trusted providers needed to run the business, for example:

  • Website hosting (GitHub Pages)
  • Email and form delivery (including FormSubmit and Google)
  • Accounting or payment tools, if used
  • Facebook / Meta, if you enquire through Facebook or Instagram

They should only use your information to provide their service to us.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries: usually up to 12 months if we do not start work
  • Client records and invoices: as long as needed for the work and for tax/legal purposes (typically 6 years)

We then delete or anonymise it unless we must keep it.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • See the information we hold about you
  • Correct it
  • Delete it (where we do not have to keep it)
  • Restrict or object to certain uses
  • Receive a copy of it

To do this, email sales@coastlinevirtualstudio.co.uk.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

Our website is a simple site. It may use essential cookies to work properly. If we add analytics later, we will update this notice.

Marketing

We will not send you marketing emails unless you have asked us to, or it is a reasonable follow-up to an enquiry. You can ask us to stop at any time.

Changes

We may update this notice from time to time. The latest version will be on this page.
